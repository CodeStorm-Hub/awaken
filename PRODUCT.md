# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a morning alarm they cannot casually dismiss, plus users who track workouts and territory runs as part of the same app. They open the app to set alarms, complete the required movement to stop the alarm, review streaks and stats, and optionally compete on territory and leaderboard screens.

## Product Purpose

Make waking up and staying consistent feel enforceable, playful, and measurable. The product turns the alarm into a physical task, records completed sessions, and gives users a sense of progress through streaks, summaries, and territory competition.

## Brand Personality

Direct, high-energy, competitive.

3-word personality: disciplined, neon, motivating.

## Anti-references

Generic fitness dashboard UI, soft wellness aesthetics, flat grayscale admin screens, cluttered gamified reward walls, and anything that makes the alarm feel optional or secondary.

## Design Principles

- Make the primary action unmistakable on every screen.
- Keep the user oriented during stressful moments like alarm dismissal and run tracking.
- Use feedback to reward progress immediately and clearly.
- Let competition feel motivating, not noisy or confusing.
- Preserve a strong visual identity across product modes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and clear tap targets. Keep critical actions obvious, preserve fallbacks when camera or location access fails, and avoid relying on motion or color alone to communicate state.
